# ☣️ **Industrial Disaster Prediction & Risk Classification System**

> **A machine learning-based system for analyzing environmental and industrial parameters to provide early warning and risk classification for potential disasters.**

## 🏭 **Project Overview**

The system analyzes critical industrial and environmental parameters such as **chemical exposure, air quality, radiation, temperature, humidity, oil presence, fog, and smog** to identify potential hazardous conditions. Machine learning is used to support **early warning and risk-level classification**.

## ✨ **Key Features**

- ☣️ **Chemical Exposure Analysis**
- 🌫️ **Air Quality Monitoring**
- ☢️ **Radiation Risk Analysis**
- 🌡️ **Temperature & Humidity Analysis**
- 🛢️ **Oil Presence Detection**
- 🌁 **Fog & Smog Analysis**
- ⚠️ **Early Disaster Warning**
- 📊 **Risk Level Classification**
- 🤖 **Machine Learning-Based Prediction**

## 🛠️ **Tech Stack**

**Programming & Machine Learning:** Python  
**Data Management:** Microsoft Excel

## ⚙️ **Workflow**

**Industrial & Environmental Data** 📋 → **Data Preprocessing** 🔍 → **ML Analysis** 🤖 → **Risk Classification** ⚠️ → **Early Warning** 🚨

## 🎯 **Objective**

To develop a **machine learning-based early warning system** that analyzes multiple environmental and industrial risk factors to support **proactive disaster detection and risk assessment**.
